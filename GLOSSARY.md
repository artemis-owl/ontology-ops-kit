## customer-domain `v2.1.0`

Owner: **platform-billing-squad**

### ActiveCustomer

An identity that has completed a billable transaction in the last 30 days.

**Excludes:** `Trial Users`, `Paused Subscriptions`, `Internal Test Accounts`

**Also called:** `Billed User`, `Current Subscriber`

**System of record:** `postgres.billingdb.public.subscriptions` where `billing_status='active'`

### TrialUser

An identity inside a time-boxed evaluation period that has never been billed.

**Excludes:** `Accounts that lapsed from a paid plan`

**Also called:** `Evaluator`

**System of record:** `postgres.billingdb.public.subscriptions` where `billing_status='trial'`

### ChurnedCustomer

An identity with no billable transaction in the last 90 days that previously had one.

**Excludes:** `Trial Users`, `Accounts closed for fraud`

**System of record:** `postgres.billingdb.public.subscriptions` where `billing_status='cancelled'`

## support-domain `v1.2.0`

Owner: **customer-operations**

### OpenTicket

A support request that has not reached a resolved or closed state.

**Excludes:** `Tickets awaiting customer reply`, `Spam-classified requests`

**Also called:** `Active Ticket`

**System of record:** `postgres.supportdb.public.tickets` where `state IN ('new','in_progress')`

### EscalatedTicket

An open ticket routed to a named engineer after breaching its first-response target.

**Excludes:** `Tickets manually flagged as priority without a breach`

**System of record:** `postgres.supportdb.public.tickets` where `escalated_at IS NOT NULL`
